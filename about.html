About

Five years in, and about to finish the degree.

When I started my first real job as a software developer, I hadn't even taken the SAT. Armed with several years of experience in the industry, I'm looking to enter the job market swinging - just as soon as I've finished college.

How it started

I started working at Solinftec via a combination of careful inquiry, personal connection, and a lucky break. All I was looking for was a foot in the door towards doing what I loved as a job. I got that - in addition to a controlled exposure to the life of a developer and the expectations I would experience in the industry.

Contango

My time at Contango saw me evolve from a mere programmer to a developer. I went from working on small-scale components to building major features with a real impact on customers from the ground up. When I left, I had years of practical experience with continuous integration, the software lifecycle, testing, and more.

School

I am currently finishing my bachelor's degree in computer science at Purdue University, West Lafayette, and will be graduating in the spring of 2027. I'm pursuing a triple concentration in software engineering, database and information systems, and machine intelligence, and I'm almost done.

How I work

I enjoy tackling open-ended problems with clear constraints - and if I have to learn a new technology or framework to solve them, so much the better. My main strength is adaptability - if it needs to be done, I can figure out how to get it done.
